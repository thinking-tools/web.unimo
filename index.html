We can’t.

Everything unimo carries between your devices is encrypted on the device before it leaves. Only your devices can read it.

This is all we ever hold.

We keep a scrambled copy that we can’t read, and neither could anyone who got into our servers. Nobody is cooking from this.

Your devices can.

The same idea WhatsApp and Signal use for messages. unimo uses it for your memos, clipboard, notifications, contacts and history.

No password.

Your fingerprint or screen lock opens unimo on your phone. There is nothing to forget, reuse or leak.

One recovery key.

You get it once, when you sign up. Keep it somewhere safe. If you lose every device it is the only way back in, because we can’t reset what we can’t open.

A new one joins with a one-time code, and only after you say yes on a device you already use. Remove a device and its copy is wiped.

Long story short.

One bar for your apps, people, memos, the web and AI, on your phone and your computer. Here is what stands behind it.

Synchronization.

What one device knows, they all know.

  • Contacts, memos, clipboardNotifications, history and settings too.
  • Android, Mac, Windows, LinuxGrapheneOS too. A web app is next.
  • You decide what travelsThe clipboard stays off until you switch it on. Notifications have their own switch.
  • It forgets on purposeThe clipboard keeps your last five items. Notifications are deleted after 24 hours.

Backups.

Lose your phone. Lose nothing.

  • Three locationsEvery change is written to three separate storage sites.
  • Two providersNo single company holds all three copies.
  • Sealed, all threeEvery copy is encrypted before it arrives. Neither we nor the providers can read it.
  • One recovery keyShown once, at sign-up. If every device is gone, it brings everything back.
ML‑KEM‑1024 ML‑DSA‑87 AES‑256‑GCM

Security.

Post-quantum encryption, end to end.

  • Sealed on the deviceWhat you sync is sealed with AES‑256‑GCM before it leaves. We only ever hold ciphertext.
  • NIST post-quantum standardsML‑KEM‑1024 for key exchange. ML‑DSA‑87 signs every change, on the device that made it.
  • No passwordOn Android your key sits in secure hardware, opened by fingerprint or screen lock.
  • You approve every deviceA new one joins with a one-time code and your yes. Remove one and its copy is wiped.

Privacy.

Private from the web. And from us.

  • On your devicesOnly your devices can read what you sync. Even their names are encrypted.
  • On our serversWhat you ask the AI is not kept. Searches are not tied to your account.
  • Out on the webunimo asks the web and the AI for you. They get the question, never your account or IP address.
  • Under EU lawHosted in the EU. The GDPR applies. The US CLOUD Act does not.

No ads. No trackers. No VC money.

unimo is paid for by the people who use it. 100%.

Everything is open source. Standing on the shoulders of giants.

The Android app is GPLv3, everything else MIT. These people shared their code. So do we.
