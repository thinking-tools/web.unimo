Open models.

unimo uses open-weight models. Their workings are published for anyone to examine, not sealed inside one company.

Nothing is kept.

They run with zero data retention: your question is deleted as soon as it is answered and never used for training. unimo passes it on and keeps no record of who asked what.

Hard to trick.

A web page can hide instructions meant to hijack an AI, a trick called prompt injection. unimo labels everything the AI reads with where it came from and checks those labels before anything is sent or done. When in doubt, it asks you.

How you use it

  1. On your phone, press Home and type.unimo becomes your Android home screen. To switch back, open Settings, search for “home app” and pick your old one. It takes two taps.
  2. On your computer, press the shortcut and type.The same line opens on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer.
  3. Add a device with a one-time code.It joins only after you say yes on a device you already use. Remove a device and its copy is wiped.

Questions

Do I have to give up my home screen?

On Android, unimo is the home screen. Try it for a day. To switch back, open Settings, search for “home app” and pick your old one. Nothing is lost.

Can you read my memos?

No. They are scrambled on your phone with a key we never get.

What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on a new one with your recovery key, then remove the lost phone from your account. Its copy is wiped the next time it connects.

And if I lose the recovery key too?

As long as one of your devices is still signed in, nothing is lost. Without any device and without the key, nobody can open your account, including us. That is what “only you” means.

Which devices?

Android phones, and Mac, Windows and Linux computers. iPhone is on the list.

How do you make money?

Subscriptions. No ads, no data sales.

What happens to my things if unimo shuts down?

The code is open and the server can be run by anyone.
